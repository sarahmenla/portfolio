SHADE

Your true colours, in writing.

A vision-model colour analysis. It names your colour season with a confidence score and plain-language reasoning, then scores real retailer products on shade match and on your ingredient avoid-list.

01

Skills it shows

Applied vision AI

Reads face, wrist, eye and hair photos and returns a colour season with a confidence score and its reasoning.

Confidence thresholds

Below 70% confidence it asks for a retake in daylight instead of guessing.

Ranking design

Two separate scores on real retailer catalogues: shade match, and fit with your ingredient avoid-list.

Inclusive, private by design

Tested across the full range of skin tones, with any failure blocking release. Photos are processed, deleted and never used for training.

02

Problem, build, result

1Problem

A professional colour analysis costs £150–£500. Free apps name a season with no reasoning, and nothing you can use in a shop.

2What I built

A reading, a 30-shade palette made from it, and a filter that scores real products against both the palette and your avoid-list.

3Result

A live product that shows its working and states its confidence, built and shipped inside the hackathon day.

70%confidence floor before it answers
30shades in every palette
2separate scores per product
